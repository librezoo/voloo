DC supply → voloo → device

USB-C connects to the computer for control. voloo is not a power supply.

Red to + , Black to −. Color is only an aid: verify actual polarity before wiring.

Connect a wire: press → insert → release

Disconnect power first. Strip 11 mm of insulation as specified by the terminal manufacturer. Keep the strands together.

Press the orange tab

Press toward the circuit board to open the clamp.

Insert the wire

Push the conductor fully into the clamp.

Release and check

Check that the wire is secure and no bare copper is exposed.

The orange tab opens the wire clamp. It does not switch power.
To remove a wire, disconnect all power, hold the matching orange tab and gently pull the wire straight out. Don't force it.

Nominal operating range

DC input voltage 3.3–28 V
Maximum recommended current 3 A

Power P = V × I; 28 V × 3 A ≈ 84 W.
That figure applies only at 28 V and 3 A. It is not a general power rating.

Current above 3 A can damage the hardware.
Set a current limit on the external supply and allow for startup current.

Valid VIN > 30.5 V: output OFF; overvoltage protection latched.
Valid VIN < 30.0 V: latch clears automatically; output stays OFF.

A new explicit ON command is required to restore power.
No extra overvoltage reset command is needed.

Before power-on

Check polarity and make sure the device and computer use separate supplies.

  • Reversed polarity damages the hardware. Red +, black −. Verify supply polarity, then turn the supply OFF and follow the physical markings.
  • Use independent supplies for the device and USB host computer. Use separate supplies for the device and computer. Different ports on one adapter don't count.
  • The device takes power only from OUT. Disconnect other power sources.
  • OFF ≠ 0 V. To remove residual charge, use discharge and confirm VOUT ≤ 1 V.
